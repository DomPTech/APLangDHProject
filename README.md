# AP Lang Digital History Project — Tennessee Valley Authority (TVA)

## Overview
This repository contains our AP Language & Composition Digital History Project focused on the Tennessee Valley Authority (TVA) and its impact on Tennessee during the 20th century.

The project combines historical research, digital humanities methodology, and modern web development to create an interactive educational website. It explores:

- Life in the Appalachian region before the TVA  
- The Tennessee River’s influence on early communities  
- The arrival of the TVA during the New Deal  
- Electrification, modernization, and economic transformation  
- The long‑term legacy of the TVA  

This project was created as part of a 27‑student AP Lang Digital Humanities initiative.

## Website Structure

### `index.html` — Home Page  
A scrolling digital exhibit featuring:
- The Appalachian Town  
- The River’s Edge  
- TVA Arrival  
- Impact on Tennessee  
- Legacy of the TVA  

Includes animations, section navigation, and interactive elements.

### `research.html` — Research Process  
Breaks down:
- Research methodology  
- Primary sources  
- Digital tools  
- Workflow  
- Reflection  

### `sources.html` — Bibliography  
A complete list of:
- Primary sources  
- Secondary sources  
- Digital archives  
- Maps, images, and government documents  

### `about.html` — About the Project  
Information about the project team and assignment context.
